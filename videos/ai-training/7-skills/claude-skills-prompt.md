**File:** `build_skills.py` -> `claude-skills.excalidraw`
**Curriculum ref:** Day 7 of the AI training day-by-day series. **No Day 7 entry exists

## Where it came from

This board **moved here from `videos/claude/4-skills/`** (`build_skills_training.py` ->
`skills.excalidraw`) and was **cut from eleven beats to six** when the day-by-day series
became the authoritative one - the same move Day 4 made out of `videos/claude/2-projects/`.
`videos/claude/` now has a second numbering gap, at 4. A gap is not lost work.

The old eleven-beat board was the Module 2 feature walkthrough: what a Skill is, what is
inside a `SKILL.md`, the four types, Skills versus other features, how to enable one,
how to make one, do-and-avoid, recap. Day 7 is a different video with a different job -
it teaches the *habit*, not the feature surface. Everything displaced is on
`claude-skills-resource-card.md`; see "What came off the board" below.

**Why this day exists:** the cheapest rung on the ladder and the first real artefact a
person ships. Placed before Cowork deliberately - most tasks people bring to Cowork
should have been a Skill.

**Ships:** one Skill published to the gallery, tested on a known input.
**Pause-and-try:** find the correction you have typed most often this month.

## How it is built

One flowing hand-drawn board, generated by `build_skills.py`, which imports the shared
engine (`from excalidraw_kit import *`) and holds only the composition: the beat scaffold
(`OX` / `WID` / `ACCENT` / `beat_head`) and this board's bespoke illustrations. Never
hand-place elements in the `.excalidraw`; change the build script and regenerate. Never
edit `excalidraw_kit.py`.

```bash
python3 videos/ai-training/7-skills/build_skills.py     # rebuild this board
/usr/bin/python3 preview.py videos/ai-training/7-skills/claude-skills.excalidraw out.png
/usr/bin/python3 preview.py <scene> out.png XMIN XMAX   # close-up on one beat
python3 build_all.py                                    # rebuild all + Style-B guard
```

`preview.py` needs `/usr/bin/python3` - Pillow is on the system interpreter, not
Homebrew's. Beat *n* frames at `XMIN = (n-1)*2000 - 100`, `XMAX = (n-1)*2000 + 1250`.

`finish()` self-checks palette discipline, the hand font, absence of frames, text
overflow and text/text collisions before writing. The board builds with **0 collisions**
and `build_all.py` prints **GUARD PASS**. `random.seed(70714)` keeps re-runs identical.

## Layout

Six beats, one uniform slot each - content ~1120 x 980 (~1.15 : 1), `GAP = 800` of
whitespace between slots. You record by panning left to right and framing one beat at a
time on a 14" laptop; the whitespace is the camera. No two neighbouring beats share an
accent, so every pan lands on a fresh colour.

| # | Accent | Heading on the board | What is drawn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | orange | you have explained this four times | The same correction typed into four separate chat doodles, each tilted. Chip: "the fourth time is the signal". |
| 2 | violet | procedure, not context | Side by side and sharp. A pale violet Project folder holding background against a crisp indigo Skill card holding numbered steps. Caption on the divide: "context is what it knows, a Skill is what it does". |
| 3 | blue | search the gallery first | The gallery as two shelves with things already on them, including the built-in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF Skills. Chip: "someone has already built this". **Carries the red data caution.** |
| 4 | teal | the hidden assumption | The step you never wrote down, drawn as a dashed hole in a numbered list that a tumbling colleague falls straight into. |
| 5 | red | make it critique its own steps | The prompt-repair beat, absorbed from the retired reverse-prompting day. Demo badge. |
| 6 | green | test on a known input | Same input in, known answer out, run before and after the edit. Close chip: "a Skill nobody else can run is a note to self". |

**Pan order:** left to right, beats 1 to 6, one slot at a time. Documented in the build
script's module docstring.

## Decisions made while building, and why

**Two colours are held constant across the board and are never beat accents.**
`INDIGO` = a Skill, wherever the object appears (beats 2, 3, 6). `VIOLET` = a Project
folder, the repo-wide convention shared with the Day 4 board. On beat 2 both appear at
once, so the Project folder is filled with the pale `VIOLET_T` tint and the Skill card is
crisp white with a saturated header - reference material reads passive, the procedure
reads active. That weight difference, not hue alone, is what makes the beat "sharp".

**One worked example runs through beats 2, 4, 5 and 6** - a monthly report Skill with
four steps, defined once as `SKILL_STEPS` in the build script. The hidden assumption
("we always exclude the trial accounts") is the hole in beat 4, the first thing Claude
flags in beat 5, and the edit that turns £5,020 into £4,180 in beat 6. Keep them in
step: change `SKILL_STEPS` and the beat 5 findings and beat 6 cards must move with it.
Figures are fictional and the scenario is deliberately everyday business.

**The data caution stayed on the board, on beat 3.** It was beat 10 of the old eleven-beat
board ("never put confidential or sensitive data in a Skill file - they're shared"). The
six-beat brief has no slot for it, but this is a mandatory course for a regulated
pharmacy and dropping the only on-screen data-handling rule to a Resource Card would not
survive clinical-safety review. Beat 3 is where sharing becomes real - a shelf of things
other people published - so it lives there, in red, with a caution diamond.

**Beat 5 teaches critique, not interview.** The board and the script say "you are asking
it to critique what you wrote - not to write it for you." That is the officially
supported half of reverse prompting. **Do not add "have Claude interview you" to this
board or script as doctrine**, and note that the board deliberately does not name it -
naming a practice in order to warn against it teaches it.

**Product mechanics are limited to what the old board already claimed.** The brief gave
no source list, unlike the Day 3 and Day 4 briefs which both do. Beat 3 uses the brief's
word "gallery"; the old board said "Skills directory" and "Browse skills" in the enable
flow. **The board now standardises on "gallery"** - check that against the current
product surface before recording. Gallery contents, plan availability and the
code-execution prerequisite date faster than anything else here; the prerequisite has
moved to the Resource Card rather than onto the board, because it changes what a person
*clicks*, not what they *type*.

**Pause-and-try and "Ships" are script blocks, not extra beats.** Six beats, as specified.
The 10s hook / 30s why / pause-and-try / 15s close structure is a shape for the narration
and is carried in `claude-skills-script.md`.

## Non-negotiables

- **Frameless.** No Excalidraw `frame` elements (a hard Style-B guard failure - it is the
  rejected Style A's signature). No boxes round beats, no connector spine, no inter-beat
  arrows, no colour-band washes, white background. "Separate frames for the video" means
  separate beat slots.
- All text in the Virgil hand font via the kit (`fontFamily: 1`), `roughness: 1`,
  `lineHeight: 1.4`. Headings via the kit's `heading()` - no step-number circle, no
  highlighter sweep behind a title, no decorative sparkles. Body and explanation text
  stays ink/grey; accents carry headings, labels, shapes and fills.
- Lively Excalidraw palette only, colour-coded per beat. Colour comes from vivid
  elements, not from background washes behind beats.
- Charming primitive illustrations over emoji. Light rotation jitter on notes and chips -
  this board uses a local `jit_min()` rather than the kit's `jit()` where four copies of
  one doodle sit together, because `jit()` can land near zero and one untilted copy reads
  as a mistake.
- Bespoke one-off illustrations (`mini_chat`, `shelf_card`, `falling_person`,
  `dashed_box`, `skill_card`, `rotate_group`) live in this build script, not in the kit.
  A kit edit re-styles every video and is a deliberate, rare act.
- **Examples generic, not Phlo-specific.** Everyday business scenarios only. No branding
  or logo, no patient or clinical content, fictional identifiers and figures only.
  Phlo-native material belongs in the live cut-away on beat 5, not on the board.
- Product proper nouns exact: Claude, Artifact, Project, Skill, MCP, Connector, Cowork,
  Claude Desktop, Claude Tag, Dispatch.
- British English, hyphens not em dashes, no Oxford commas.
- The live cut-away is marked with `demo_badge(...)` on beat 5 and with
  `[CUT TO CLAUDE DESKTOP]` / `[BACK TO BOARD]` in `claude-skills-script.md`.
